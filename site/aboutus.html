About us

A family trade, three decades strong.

Pillar Construction started with a family drywall business and a reputation for doing things right. That hasn’t changed.

Our story

We grew up on the job.

We started out as kids working in our father’s family drywall business in the early ’80s. In 1993 we went out on our own and formed Pillar Construction, and it wasn’t long before we were known as the best drywall company around.

We incorporated in 2000 as Pillar Drywall, Inc., keeping our trade name, Pillar Construction, and went on to work in more than 13 counties across New Jersey. When the economy turned around 2008, we branched out. Now, along with drywall, we offer a full range of home remodeling services, all with the same eye for detail.
